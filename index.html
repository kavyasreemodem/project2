Scientific Methodology

How AgriSmart AI Evaluates Crops

A transparent, deterministic agronomy decision model based on ICAR standards and agricultural science.

01

Soil Compatibility

Checks physical soil texture (Alluvial, Black, Clay, Loam, Sandy) against the crop's root anchorage and drainage needs.

02

Climate & Temperature

Matches ambient seasonal temperature, humidity bounds, and precipitation thresholds with crop phenology.

03

Water Availability

Cross-references farm irrigation infrastructure and rainfall to prevent moisture deficit or waterlogging stress.

04

Rotation Synergy

Applies agronomic rotation rules (+10% for legumes following cereals, penalty for repeating exhaustive monocultures).

Transparent Decision Matrix Weights

Every crop score is deterministically calculated using these agronomic weights:

Soil Texture (25%)
Evaluates root aeration, soil moisture retention, and tillage requirements.
Climate & Rainfall (25%)
Compares temperature comfort zone, relative humidity, and precipitation tolerance.
Water Capacity (20%)
Guarantees crop water demand matches available canal or borewell supply.
Growing Season (15%)
Confirms photoperiod and temperature alignment (Kharif, Rabi, or Zaid).
Soil pH Balance (10%)
Ensures soil acidity/alkalinity does not lock essential micronutrients.
Rotation Synergy (±10%)
Rewards nitrogen-fixing legume transitions and penalizes pest-accumulating monocultures.
